About

Based in Maui, Alex brings live saxophone to luxury weddings, corporate gatherings, and private celebrations throughout Hawaii. Every performance is delivered with artistry, professionalism, and joy.

His pursuit of music has taken him across the country and onto stages alongside Pat Simmons of the Doobie Brothers, Mick Fleetwood, and Jason Arcilla, onto the same bill as John Legend, and to performances at exclusive events with A-list guests.

Whether you're envisioning a tender ballad for your ceremony, a sophisticated atmosphere during dinner, or a high-energy dance floor for your reception, Alex will bring his experience and talent to fulfill your vision.
