About

Alex is a professional saxophonist based in Maui, specializing in luxury weddings, corporate events, and exclusive private celebrations. His career has taken him across the country and onto stages alongside Pat Simmons of the Doobie Brothers, Mick Fleetwood, and Jason Arcilla, onto the same bill as John Legend, and to performances at exclusive events with many A-list guests.

Whether you're envisioning a romantic ceremony, an elegant dinner atmosphere, or a high-energy dance floor, Alex brings the experience and talent to elevate your vision.
